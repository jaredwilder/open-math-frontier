# 3. Generalized seven-point block lemma

\[

\[
E_m=2\sum_{1\le i<j\le m}w(y_j-y_i).

\[
\boxed{

}
\]

The counting reason is exact:

- a pair spanning `r` consecutive gaps appears in at most `7-r` seven-point windows;
- this cancels the local coefficient `2/(7-r)`;
- each single gap appears in at most six windows.

\[
