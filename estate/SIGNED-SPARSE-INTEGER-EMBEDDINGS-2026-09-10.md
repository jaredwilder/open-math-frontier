# Signed Sparse Integer Embeddings from Shifted B_s Sets

**Author:** Jared Wilder  
**Public release:** 2026-09-10

This note extracts the standalone mathematics from the August theorem-recipe program. It deliberately omits the later packet/capsule/distributed implementation layer that the estate places behind the IP firewall.

Historical novelty is **not** claimed. The inputs are close to classical `B_h` / generalized Sidon sequence theory, dissociated sets, and sparse integer encodings. The value of this release is the explicit signed formulation and its proof skeleton.

## SGN-04 — Shifted B_s encoding for signed ternary sparse states

Let

\[
a_1,\dots,a_m\in\{0,\dots,N-1\}
\]

be a `B_s` set modulo `N`, in the sense that equal sums of `s` terms imply equality of the corresponding multisets.

Set

\[
L=2s(N-1)+1
\]

and

\[
w_i=L+a_i.
\]

Then the map

\[
\Phi(x)=\sum_{i=1}^m x_iw_i
\]

is injective on

\[
\{x\in\{-1,0,1\}^m:\ |\operatorname{supp}x|\le s\}.
\]

### Proof core

Suppose

\[
\sum_i x_iw_i=\sum_i y_iw_i.
\]

Move terms to one side. The coefficient of the common shift `L` is the difference in total signed charge. The residual contribution from the `a_i` is too small in magnitude to cancel one nonzero multiple of

\[
L=2s(N-1)+1.
\]

Therefore the total signed charges agree.

After moving negative terms across the equality, the remaining collision is an equality between two sums of the same number `k` of `a_i` terms, with `k<=s`. Pad both sides to `s` terms and invoke the `B_s` uniqueness property. The multisets are identical, which recovers equality of the signed coefficient vectors.

## SGN-05 — Optimal exponent for signed ternary sparse encoding

For fixed `s`, consider positive integer weights

\[
1\le w_1,\dots,w_m\le W
\]

that must encode every signed ternary vector of support at most `s` injectively through

\[
x\mapsto\sum_i x_iw_i.
\]

Then the least possible order of the maximum weight is

\[
\boxed{\Theta_s(m^s)}.
\]

### Lower bound

The number of signed support-at-most-`s` states is

\[
\sum_{r=0}^{s}2^r\binom mr
=\Theta_s(m^s).
\]

All encoded sums lie in an interval of length at most `2sW+1`, so injectivity forces

\[
W=\Omega_s(m^s).
\]

### Upper bound

A classical `B_s` construction in a cyclic group of order

\[
O_s(m^s)
\]

combined with SGN-04 gives positive integer weights of maximum size

\[
O_s(m^s).
\]

Thus

\[
W=\Theta_s(m^s).
\]

## SGN-06 — Three-sparse corollary

At `s=3`, the optimal maximum weight satisfies

\[
\boxed{W=\Theta(m^3)}.
\]

This is just the specialization of SGN-05.

## SGN-07 — General bounded coefficients

Fix integers `A,s>=1`. For vectors

\[
x\in[-A,A]^m\cap\mathbb Z^m
\]

with support at most `s`, a charge-separation argument combined with a `B_{As}` set gives a constructive family of positive integer weights with maximum

\[
\boxed{O_{A,s}(m^{As})}
\]

that encodes every such vector injectively.

The exponent here is a constructive upper bound from the archived theorem program; unlike SGN-05, this note does not claim it is optimal for every `A,s`.
