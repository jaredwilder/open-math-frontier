## 2. Mission

Mine `github.com/jaredwilder` for the largest defensible mathematical novelty and publication headlines. The program is cumulative and adversarial against *underclaiming*.

The objective is **not** to prove that results are unimportant. Prior art establishes the baseline and identifies Jared Wilder's exact delta: new theorem, bound, range, classification, proof, formalization, computation, bridge, obstruction, equality case, conceptual coordinate, or stronger theorem scope.

The September estate-forensics history and Novelty Hunter sweeps are one program. Never fork them again.

## 3. Mandatory live-delta gate

The estate changes during audits. Every substantive pass must first identify public commits newer than the Novelty Hunter checkpoint across the high-value subject homes and release indices.

Classify each live delta as one of:

- new mathematical theorem/result;
- authority upgrade of an existing theorem;
- publication/routing only;
- correction/quarantine;
- computational evidence only;
- open target / research state.

A commit title is not theorem authority. Open the theorem source.

## 4. Claim discipline

- Distinguish `READY`, `PAPER`, `COMPUTATIONAL PAPER`, `SEAL`, `MINE`, `KNOWN/REDISCOVERY`, and `QUARANTINE`.
- Never promote a bounded computation to an infinite theorem.
- Never demote a valid child theorem merely because the famous parent problem is open.
- Separate mathematical validity from historical priority.
- For computational results, demand reproducible verifier/certificate evidence.
- For formal results, inspect the actual theorem statement and axiom footprint; a clean kernel gate does not by itself prove semantic relevance.
- Inspect unused hypotheses and theorem names: formal statements may be mathematically stronger than their campaign framing.
- **Source theorem/paper authority outranks findings-ledger metadata.** If a ledger says `PROVED` but its cited source says `schema`, `hypothesis`, `UNKNOWN`, or leaves dependencies open, use the source status.
- Preserve corrections and negative results as regression tests, but do not let them dominate a novelty-ranking pass.
- For RH/Weil material, preserve the explicit boundary: auxiliary method-obstruction mathematics only; **no RH proof/disproof claim**.

- live commit differential;
