## Evidence classes

| class | meaning |
|---|---|
| **KERNEL-VERIFIED** | Lean accepted the stated declaration under the disclosed trust boundary. Formal validity and source-problem fidelity remain separate questions. |
| **PROVED / EXACT** | A conventional proof or an exact finite computation establishes the stated scope. |
| **CANDIDATE** | A serious reproduced argument has at least one load-bearing dependency outside the strongest available seal. |
| **COMPUTATIONAL FRONTIER** | Exhaustive or large-scale search to an explicit boundary. |
| **WORKING RECORD / CORPUS** | Ore, attack logs, statement formalizations, proof-engineering material, or provenance with heterogeneous internal authority. |
| **RETRACTED / CORRECTED** | A claim or route later found unsound or superseded and deliberately preserved with its correction. |

The rule is simple: **the theorem statement says what was established; the evidence class says how strongly; the audit trail says what failed. None substitutes for the others.**

# IX. Release doctrine

1. **Result first; evidence immediately attached.** State the strongest surviving mathematical object before discussing a larger claim it never made.
2. **No result is upgraded by proximity.** A theorem does not promote a neighboring conjecture, and a finite search does not prove beyond its boundary.
3. **No result is downgraded by parent-problem status.** A larger unsolved problem does not make a proved child theorem, exact reduction, or finite classification less proved.
4. **Formal validity and semantic fidelity are separate gates.** Kernel acceptance establishes the formal statement as written; source equivalence requires its own audit.
5. **Exact scope travels with the claim.** Finite, asymptotic, conditional, computational, countable, continuum, candidate, and source-equivalent are different labels.
6. **Negative results and corrections remain public.** They belong to the audit trail, not as rhetorical headlines for unrelated positive mathematics.
7. **Archive size is not theorem count.** Raw events, duplicates, workflow labels, formal declarations, and promoted mathematical objects are reported in separate categories.
8. **Pure mathematics is open; load-bearing applied IP remains behind the firewall unless deliberately cleared.**

# XI. Invitation to falsify

If a theorem statement is false, a dependency is missing, a computation does not reproduce, a novelty claim collides with prior literature, or a formal statement mismatches its source problem, open an issue in the relevant repository with the smallest reproducible objection.

The standard is not “trust the author.” It is **make the mathematical attack surface public and state exactly what survived.**
